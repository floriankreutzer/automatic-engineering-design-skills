## Credits and reuse

Keep this notice, `icons-credits.json`, the credited author, versioned source and
applicable license links with copies. Preserve attribution and identify changes
when required by the applicable license. The Taste resolver wraps and normalizes
source SVG markup; inspect the emitted SVG and record further modifications.

Credits use a conditional collection-level license label. `trademark: null` means
the icon's branded status has not been determined, not that it has no owner or
that its use has been approved. Verify the specific mark's terms before reuse.
The `file:` prefix identifies a source set and does not classify trademark status.
